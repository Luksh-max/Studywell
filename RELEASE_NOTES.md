## StudyWell release

StudyWell is an offline-first study planner and focus companion. The Android release bundles the web app assets inside the APK; it does not need a hosted website or an account to run.

### Included
- Daily mission planning, task planning and exam readiness tracking
- Focus timer with session outcomes
- Offline flashcards and review scheduling
- Mood check-ins, private journal and weekly insights
- Local reminders for tasks and focus sessions on Android
- Optional PIN access barrier, local backups and theme settings

### Privacy and platform notes
- Study data is stored on the device.
- Android notifications require the user's permission.
- Alarm delivery can be delayed by Android battery management.
- The PIN is an access barrier, not full encryption of all stored records.
