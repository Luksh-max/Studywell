# Contributing and reporting issues

StudyWell is currently published for review and personal evaluation under the terms in `LICENSE`; it is not an open-source contribution project. Please do not submit copied code, republish the source, or create derivative public repositories without permission.

If you discover a reproducible bug, you may open an issue with:

- the page or action where it happened;
- the device, Android version, and browser version;
- exact steps to reproduce it;
- what you expected and what happened;
- whether the issue also occurs offline.

Do not include private journal entries, backups, passwords, PINs, keystores, or other personal data in an issue.
